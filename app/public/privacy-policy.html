Privacy Policy – SplitBill

Last updated: February 2025

SplitBill ("we", "our", or "the app") is a bill-splitting and expense-tracking app. This policy describes how we handle your information when you use our app and services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: Email address, display name, and password (stored securely) when you create an account.
  • Profile and usage: Name, profile details, group memberships, expenses you create or participate in, and split details.
  • Receipt and bill data: Photos you take or upload for receipt scanning. Images and extracted text (items, amounts) are processed to help you split bills and are stored only as needed to provide the service.
  • Device and app usage: Device type, app version, and push notification tokens so we can send you notifications (e.g. expense reminders). We may collect analytics (e.g. crash reports or usage) to improve the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To provide bill splitting, expense tracking, groups, and sharing with other users you invite.
  • To process receipt images (including via third-party AI/OCR services) to extract items and amounts.
  • To send push notifications about expenses and groups you are part of.
  • To improve the app, fix errors, and ensure security.

3. Data storage and sharing

Your data is stored on our servers (or our service providers). We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only:

  • With other SplitBill users when you create or join groups and add expenses (as needed for the app to work).
  • With service providers that help us run the app (e.g. hosting, analytics, push notifications, AI/OCR).
  • When required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encryption and secure authentication) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we strive to protect your information to the best of our ability.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can access and update your profile and account details in the app.
  • You can delete your account or request deletion of your data; we will process such requests in line with applicable law.
  • You can disable push notifications in your device or app settings.
  • Camera and photo access are optional and used only for receipt scanning when you choose to use that feature.

6. Children

SplitBill is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy at this URL and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at the support or contact option provided in the SplitBill app or on our website.
